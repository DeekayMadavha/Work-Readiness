# Business communnicatiom
Business Communication – STAR Reflection

Situation:
In the Business Communication module, I was presented with scenarios involving workplace correspondence, tone, and professional presentation.

Task:
I needed to demonstrate how to apply appropriate communication methods in formal and informal business contexts, including email etiquette and virtual meeting conduct.

Action:
I analyzed case-based questions and applied best practices for written and verbal communication, focusing on professionalism, clarity, and respect. I also learned how visual and non-verbal cues affect business interactions.

Result:
I scored 100%, which highlights my ability to communicate effectively in diverse business settings. The module reinforced the importance of tone, format, and professionalism in maintaining workplace credibility and trust.

# Interview skills
Interview Skills – STAR Reflection

Situation:
The Interview Skills module placed me in scenarios where I had to identify best practices for interviews and self-presentation.

Task:
I needed to demonstrate an understanding of interview techniques, such as using the CAR method, handling tough questions, and showcasing my strengths effectively.

Action:
I practiced answering questions using structured approaches, researched effective body language, and learned to align my responses with company values and job expectations.

Result:
I achieved a perfect score of 100%. The module enhanced my confidence in interviews by showing how preparation, clarity, and reflection on past experiences help convey competence and professionalism.

# Professional Networking
Professional Networking – STAR Reflection

Situation:
In the Professional Networking module, I explored how to build and sustain relationships that contribute to career development and mutual growth.

Task:
The goal was to understand networking concepts, such as the golden rule of networking, how to approach events, and how to avoid common mistakes.

Action:
I studied the importance of giving value before expecting it in return and practiced identifying opportunities to connect meaningfully with others. I also focused on developing an authentic professional presence.

Result:
I scored 100%, which reflects a solid understanding of networking principles. I learned that effective networking is based on genuine interest, consistent communication, and professional integrity.

# Workplace Etiquette

Workplace Etiquette – STAR Reflection

Situation:
During the Workplace Etiquette module, I encountered realistic workplace scenarios requiring appropriate responses to professional communication, time management, and ethical behavior.

Task:
My objective was to demonstrate understanding of professional conduct, such as handling rude emails, managing punctuality, maintaining confidentiality, and adhering to workplace policies.

Action:
I reviewed each question carefully, applied reasoning based on workplace standards, and reflected on the importance of professionalism and respect in daily communication. I also analyzed what an ideal employee response would be in each case.

Result:
I achieved an overall score of 80%, showing good awareness of workplace expectations. The module reinforced how small actions—such as responding calmly to offensive messages or arriving early for meetings—impact overall professionalism and team respecue
